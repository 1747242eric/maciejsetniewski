Maciej Igor Setniewski — Interior Design Director, Lead8

Maciej Igor Setniewski is an architect and interior design director specializing in experiential commercial interiors and urban design.

Philosophy

Setniewski champions experiential interior design — turning retail and commercial spaces from points of purchase into immersive destinations that forge emotional, social, and sensory connections.

Influences

His work reflects functionalism and minimalism, drawing from Ludwig Mies van der Rohe and Louis Sullivan.
